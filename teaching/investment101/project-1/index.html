Project 1 · U.S. Household Balance Sheet

This demonstration uses Federal Reserve Financial Accounts (Z.1) data to construct the 2026 Q1 balance sheet of U.S. households and nonprofit organizations. The script selects the quarter, maps the series codes to account names, checks accounting identities, and exports a CSV with amounts in billions of dollars.
